Glance French

A new French word on your home screen, all day long.

Glance French is an Android app for learning French: a home-screen widget that shows a word, its meaning and an example, flashcards that come back just before you forget, and a course from A1 to B2 that prepares you for the DELF, the TEF and the TCF.

No account, no ads, no tracking: your progress stays on your phone.
